My Growth in CS 📈

Skill Development

This page showcases areas of my growth.

My Unit 3 Project, Pet Pals, represents a huge area of growth for me in the structure of code and management of multiple interactive elements at once. When we first started this unit, I found it hard to organize big projects with large strings of code like this project. I managed to break down my game’s logic with custom functions like decayStats(), drawUI() and checkButtons() so that my draw loop stayed clean and easy to navigate. It was hard for me to master the changing of states and the animations. With the three stat meters, getting them to balance so they could decrease over time while still triggering the animations of the corgi required careful fine tuning and a lot of debugging. One more obstacle I faced was the bounce velocities to work smoothly with no glitching. You can still hardly even tell it’s there. I overcame these obstacles by testing each small section of code one at a time. I had to pay a lot of attention to the course in order to successfully create the project. I carefully planned my sprites, variables, and UI layout before jumping into gamelab, which helped a lot. I spent less time being confused and more time loving my corgi. Knowing what I know now, the main improvement I would make would be changing the starting screen to look more presentable. I would also add sound effects on each button click and add a custom shop for players to buy their pets new things.
